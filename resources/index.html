Personal Finance Planner

An independent Excel workbook for understanding income, expenses, monthly financial activity and the bigger picture through an updating dashboard.

Track what comes in and what goes out, use drop-down menus when entering income and expenses, review monthly metrics that update with your entries, and build a history for future planning.

  • Monthly financial metrics that update as information is entered
  • An automatically updating dashboard
  • Drop-down menus for income and expense tracking
  • Monthly and annual reviews plus assets, debts, and accounts
