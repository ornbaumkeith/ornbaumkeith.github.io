A partnership with purpose

Build your next chapter together.

Pairvoy brings individual goals and shared plans into one workspace. Make room for health, learning, adventures, and the milestones that matter to both of you.

Plan the next step, put it on the calendar, encourage your partner, and see how your effort adds up. XP, levels, and achievements add game-inspired motivation to real progress.

The app is designed around couples, with support for a core team of up to three and separately connected circles.

From intention to action

Plan it. Show up. Celebrate it.

Meaningful SMART goals

Define your goal, why it matters, ownership, target date and measure of success. Filter by owner, category, status and year.

Steps that fit your plan

Start with four editable planning suggestions. Add details, set dates, restore suggestions or create your own steps.

Everyday follow-through

Schedule actions, check them off and track progress. Completing the planning steps completes a goal and awards its existing bonus.

A shared calendar

See deadlines, step dates, actions and events. Export a calendar snapshot in .ics format for your preferred calendar.

Momentum you can see

Review XP, levels, achievements, progress charts and recent moments. Reflect on each goal month by month.

Encouragement with boundaries

Connect with other circles and choose whether to share steps, actions and reflections. Working notes stay with your core team.

Your workspace, your boundaries

Personal and shared goal permissions, connected-circle visibility and section-sharing controls are enforced by the API. Connected circles cannot edit original goals. Working notes remain within the core team.

A careful transition

The rebrand includes migration of saved settings, cached workspaces and sessions. Existing goals and preferences remain available as you move to the new name.

Testing builds are not a claim of production certification. Physical-device acceptance, recovery email delivery and operational backup checks remain release considerations.
