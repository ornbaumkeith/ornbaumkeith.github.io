A partnership with purpose

Build your next chapter together.

Pairvoy brings individual goals and shared plans into one workspace. Make room for health, learning, adventures, and the milestones that matter to both of you.

Plan the next step, put it on the calendar, encourage your partner, and see how your effort adds up. XP, levels, and achievements add game-inspired motivation to real progress.

The app is designed around couples, with support for a core team of up to three and separately connected circles.

What you can do in Pairvoy

Decide what to work on today

Start on Home with Today’s focus and My checklist. See personal actions and overdue items, mark actions complete, and keep private notes on this device.

Plan personal and shared goals

Use SMART planning to clarify why a goal matters, when it’s due and how you’ll measure success. Break it into editable steps and dated actions, then filter the collection to find what you’re working on.

Put plans on the calendar

See scheduled actions and goal dates together. Android can add events and reminders to an available device calendar with permission. Windows exports calendar events and reminders as .ics files.

See how your effort adds up

Journey tracks XP, levels, achievements and recent milestones. Activity shows progress charts and goal reflections so you can see how your habits change over time.

Make time to reflect

Open the current week’s private reflection from Home and optionally link it to a goal. Monthly goal reflections are also available from the Journey and Activity views.

Share within your Circle

Work with your core team and choose what to share with connected circles. Use Profile to make the workspace your own, switch between light and dark appearance, and manage preferences.

New in the web app · 0.5.20

  • Dual-ring progress. The purple ring tracks active goal milestones regardless of deadlines; the teal ring tracks scheduled weekly actions independently.
  • Custom rewards. Add a reward to a shared goal and choose the milestone that unlocks it. Keep it private or share it with connected circles after you earn it.
  • Updated Home. Awards & Rewards includes a direct reward shortcut. Rounded-square profile badges and subtle theme-aware outlines improve readability.

Your workspace, your boundaries

Personal and shared goal permissions, connected-circle visibility and section-sharing controls are enforced by the API. Connected circles cannot edit original goals. Shared goal working notes stay within the core team. Private action notes and weekly reflections stay on the device.

A careful transition

The rebrand includes migration of saved settings, cached workspaces and sessions. Existing goals and preferences remain available as you move to the new name.

Testing builds are not a claim of production certification. Physical-device acceptance, recovery email delivery and operational backup checks remain release considerations.
