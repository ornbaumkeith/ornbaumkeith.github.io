Small steps. Stronger families.

Family life gets busy. Good intentions can disappear between school, work, sports and household responsibilities. Pairvoy for Family gives parents and children a simple place to turn those intentions into everyday actions, check in with each other, and celebrate what they achieve.

Build responsibility

Give each family member age-appropriate commitments and a clear next step, without comparing siblings by workload.

Make time for connection

Work toward shared experiences, establish healthy routines and reflect on what matters together.

Keep big goals manageable

Break reading, savings, fitness, schoolwork and household goals into small dated actions.

Celebrate effort, not perfection

Use shared quests, achievements and experience rewards to recognize participation. A missed task is an opportunity to adjust, not a reason to compete.

What your family can do

Set personal and shared goals

Work toward something of your own or choose a family goal, like hiking four local trails. Each goal includes five SMART planning fields and four planning checkpoints.

Give everyone a next step

Create local profiles, schedule one-time or recurring daily actions, and follow the shared daily family quest. The calendar’s timeline and agenda bring upcoming goals and tasks into view.

See what’s moving forward

See completed actions alongside progress toward the outcome. Open an interactive family Orbit member preview, review the weekly shared recap, and write private task notes and reflections.

Celebrate effort together

Earn XP, explore eight achievement types, and customize experience rewards such as movie nights and family outings. Participation reflects each person’s own planned commitments for the week.

Make it personal

Customize local profile colors, emoji, initials, icons, male or female characters, hair, eyes and skin colors. Choose light or dark appearance.

Explore together

Open the interactive circular Orbit to see visible upcoming commitments. Use All, Mine and Ours filters to focus on the relevant goals.

Keep a personal journal

Add task notes and weekly reflections. These are scoped to local preview profiles, not authenticated private accounts.

Try a fictional family

Enter an isolated five-person demo with 182 historical days, five scheduled tasks a day across the household, and two weeks of upcoming commitments. Reset or exit the demo without replacing the normal workspace.

How to get started

  1. Create local family profiles.
  2. Choose a personal or shared goal and define its SMART plan.
  3. Add one-time or recurring daily actions.
  4. Check Today's Moves and the shared family quest.
  5. Celebrate milestones and review the weekly recap together.

Build details and dependencies

Pairvoy for Family uses the same ORBIT interface across its configured builds. A shared rules engine validates changes to goals, milestones, and profiles before saving them to the device.

Web
Expo and Metro produce a static web build. React Native Web displays the ORBIT interface in an isolated iframe. The browser can download a JSON snapshot and an iCalendar file for visible goals and tasks.
Windows x64
Electron wraps the static web build. The unsigned per-user NSIS installer includes an upgrade and data-retention notice. Actual upgrade preservation is awaiting Windows acceptance testing.
Android
Expo renders ORBIT in a native WebView. The testing profile targets an internal APK with Android SDK 36 configured for compilation and targeting. The Android export action sends text to the system share sheet.

Project facts. Pairvoy for Family is developed separately from Pairvoy for couples and has its own local data store. It includes nine built-in goal categories, including Health, Learning, Home, and Finance, plus adult-managed custom categories. The source is maintained in a private repository with an MIT license file.

Privacy and local storage

Goals, actions, and profiles live on the device. Use sample data during testing, especially for child profiles. Separate sign-ins, invitations, and cross-device sync are planned for a future release.
