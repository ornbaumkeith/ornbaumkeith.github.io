Pairvoy for Family

Give everyone in the family a goal to work toward and a way to help. Pick a goal, break it into small steps, and make time to see how far you’ve come together.

Inside the family workspace

What your family can do

Set personal and shared goals

Work toward something of your own or choose a family goal, like hiking four local trails. Each goal starts with four planning checkpoints, and you can add a measurable target when it helps.

Give everyone a next step

Create local profiles, assign dated actions, and see what’s due today. The calendar’s timeline and agenda bring upcoming goals and tasks into view.

See what’s moving forward

See completed actions alongside progress toward the outcome. Review the week’s activity and write a short reflection on what worked and what to try next.

Celebrate effort together

Earn XP, track streaks, unlock achievements, and set family experience rewards, like a movie night. Participation reflects each person’s own planned commitments for the week.
