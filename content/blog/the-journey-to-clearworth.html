ClearWorth is the result of more than ten years of work, learning, and personal growth coming together. The app is a new expression of interests and habits that have developed over time: understanding data, solving practical problems, improving workflows, and thinking more intentionally about finances.

A foundation built through work

My professional background spans enterprise GIS, location intelligence, databases, and automation. Working with ArcGIS Enterprise, SQL Server, and data workflows has taught me to pay attention to how information moves through a system and how people use it to make decisions.

Those lessons carry into software development. Inputs need to be understandable. Workflows need to be useful. Results need to be tested. Documentation needs to help someone use and maintain what has been built. ClearWorth draws on that foundation.

Interests that reach beyond technology

I enjoy the connection between technology and everyday problems. GIS, automation, and software design offer ways to organize complexity and make information easier to act on.

Family and years of youth sports coaching have also shaped my perspective. Preparation, communication, accountability, and persistence matter in those settings. They matter when learning something new or building a project, too. My reminder to keep going is simple: “Out work, out learn, out last!”

Personal growth and financial clarity

Personal and financial growth are ongoing efforts for me. I am interested in understanding where money goes, how expenses fit around income, and how debt and savings affect longer-term goals. Better visibility gives me a stronger starting point for those decisions.

That interest shaped the questions behind ClearWorth: What is due next? How does an expense fit into a pay period? What does the month look like? How do everyday decisions connect with savings and debt goals?

The aim is to make those questions easier to work through. A useful financial tool should help bring the details together so planning can become a more consistent habit.

Building tools to help others

I wanted to do more with what I had learned by creating apps and tools that help everyday people. The problems that interest me are often familiar ones: keeping information organized, making a repeated task easier, or understanding what needs attention next.

ClearWorth is one way to put that motivation into practice. My own interest in financial clarity became an opportunity to build something that could help others organize their finances and make more informed decisions. That same purpose guides the other projects I want to share here.

Lessons that shaped the app

  • Start with the real workflow. Organize the tool around income, expenses, dates, and goals that people need to manage.
  • Make the information clear. Weekly, monthly, and annual views offer different ways to understand the same financial picture.
  • Build trust through care. Validation, backups, access controls, and documentation belong in the development process.
  • Improve through use. Testing and feedback reveal where layouts, imports, charts, and instructions need attention.
  • Keep learning. Each improvement creates another opportunity to understand the problem more deeply.

From accumulated experience to ClearWorth

Over time, technical work and personal financial interests began to connect. Experience with data and automation provided a way to build; a desire for clearer financial planning gave that work a purpose.

ClearWorth now brings income and expenses, financial calendars, savings and debt goals, projections, and charts into a local-first Windows experience. Its development continues through testing and refinement, with attention to both the underlying data and the experience of using the app.

The story is still evolving. This portfolio and blog are places to share that work, the lessons behind it, and future projects as they take shape.
