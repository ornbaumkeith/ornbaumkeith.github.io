From a spreadsheet to ClearWorth

October 23, 2026

Before ClearWorth was an application, it was a Microsoft Excel spreadsheet. Over years of trial and error I refined a workbook to organize my income, expenses, savings, assets, and debt in one place.

Starting with a real problem

Income arrives on different dates, bills have different due dates, and a single monthly number does not always show the full picture. I wanted to see the financial details by pay period and by month, while also understanding how each month fits into the year.

The workbook grows

The spreadsheet evolved into an annual summary, twelve individual monthly worksheets, and dedicated sections for assets, debts, and accounts. Tracking those records month after month creates a history that can inform the next budget and future planning decisions.

Years of changes, lessons, and improvements

The spreadsheet took years of trial and error. I tested layouts, added calculations, and reorganized information whenever the existing process could be improved. It was a practical tool for everyday use and a way to learn which financial questions mattered most.

Where ClearWorth began

Those experiences helped inspire ClearWorth. I wanted to build on the underlying workflow with a dedicated application for dashboards, planning, calendar views, income and expense tracking, and other financial tools. The spreadsheet remains its origin story—not the same product, but an important starting point.

Making useful tools available

I'm creating a Resources library for downloadable Excel templates, starting with the personal finance workbook. The file will be available when a public release has been reviewed and uploaded.
