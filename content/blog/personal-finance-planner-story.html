My Personal Finance Planner started as a spreadsheet I built and refined over years of real use. I wanted one familiar place to keep income, expenses, payment dates, and financial records together.

Practical financial tracking

The standalone Excel workbook combines a financial summary, individual monthly worksheets, and dedicated assets, debt, and accounts views. Recording transactions over time helps build useful history for reviewing expenses and planning ahead.

Years of trial and error

I adjusted the spreadsheet as I learned what worked. I organized income and expenses around pay periods, added financial summaries, and kept improving how information could be reviewed from month to month.

An independent resource

The Personal Finance Planner is a standalone Excel template, not a component of another application. The lessons from building and using it later helped inspire ClearWorth, a separate software project.

Sharing the original

The downloadable workbook will be published exactly as supplied, without modifying its formulas, charts, sample data, or formatting. The accompanying screenshots and videos are the original files, not generated replacements.

Review any example information before use. Although some worksheet fields are labeled for passwords, do not store actual credentials in an unencrypted spreadsheet.
