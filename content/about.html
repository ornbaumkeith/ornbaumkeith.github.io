Outside work

Family and coaching

I enjoy spending time with my wife and our three sons, and I’m passionate about coaching youth wrestling, baseball, and football. I love seeing a kid stick with something difficult and realize they can do it.

Coaching has taught me to prepare, communicate clearly, and give people room to learn. I bring those lessons into how I work with teams.

Personal finance and financial health

I enjoy learning about budgeting, saving, investing, and what it takes to build financial stability over time. Every two weeks, my wife and I sit down to look at what came in, what went out, and what’s coming up. It helps us talk through trade-offs and decide what we want to work toward together.

Those conversations helped shape ClearWorthPlanner. I wanted a clearer way to compare what actually happened with what we planned and see how a change might affect the months ahead.

Goals and daily habits

I like turning a big goal into something I can put on the calendar and do this week. A short walk after dinner is a good example. The walk itself is small, but doing it regularly is how a goal starts to become a habit.

I built Pairvoy so my wife and I could work on our own goals, make plans together, and encourage each other along the way. I wanted the effort to be worth celebrating while we were still working toward the result.
