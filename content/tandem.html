Better together

Make accountability part of your relationship.

Set a goal worth showing up for, break it into practical steps, and schedule the small actions that move it forward. Tandem connects personal ambition with shared responsibility, so you and your better half can see the plan, celebrate progress, and keep each other accountable.

Whether you are building healthier habits, saving for an adventure, learning something new, or working toward a shared milestone, the aim is the same: make consistent effort visible and rewarding.

Tandem applies game-theory-inspired incentives to goal setting: actions earn XP, milestones unlock progress, and shared outcomes reward working together. It is designed for Apple and Android phones and tablets, with layouts that adapt to the available space.

From intention to action

Plan it. Show up. Celebrate it.

SMART goals with a clear purpose

Define what success looks like, choose a category and owner, and set a target date. Keep personal goals alongside goals you share as a couple.

Four suggested planning steps

Start with Prep & Plan, Schedule & System, Track & Adjust, and Finish & Review. Edit the suggestions to fit your own goal and milestones.

Habits through scheduled actions

Turn a goal into dated daily or weekly tasks. Check off completed actions, review consistency, and distinguish everyday effort from completing a milestone.

Your day at a glance

Home brings active goals, weekly actions, completed steps, XP, and today’s next steps together.

Calendar & agenda

View goal dates, milestone deadlines, scheduled actions, and team events in a monthly calendar and daily agenda. Optional native reminders require device permission.

XP, levels & achievements

Earn progress through actions, completed steps, and finished goals. Journey brings level progress, achievements, and recent moments together.

Connected couples & encouragement

Invite and connect with other teams, explore the goals they share, copy inspiration into your own workspace, and send positive encouragement.

Activity & monthly reflections

Review action history and progress rhythm, then record reflections for individual goals. Use what happened to plan the next step.

Phone & tablet layouts

The source includes phone bottom navigation and wider-screen side navigation, with light and dark appearance. Native device validation remains part of release preparation.

A little rivalry. A shared win.

Compete with your better half. Grow together.

Challenge your spouse, girlfriend, boyfriend, or partner to follow through. Compare effort, keep shared goals moving, and let another couple’s progress inspire your own. Friendly competition gives you a reason to show up; accountability helps you keep going.

Make the next action count.

The reviewed scoring model awards 2 XP for a completed action, 10 XP for a completed step, and 50 XP for a completed goal. A shared completed goal adds a 25 XP team bonus. Each 200 XP advances a level. These are development rules and may change before release.

Invite another couple into the journey.

Connected circles can view shared goals, copy ideas, and encourage one another. Agree on your own friendly challenge—such as following through on a week of planned actions—and use Tandem’s progress and activity views to stay accountable.

The supplied source does not establish a ranked cross-couple leaderboard or tournament system. Connected teams can view and copy shared goals; they cannot edit another team’s originals.

Privacy & trust

Clear boundaries for shared progress.

Your team and your connected circles

Teammates collaborate on team goals. The source filters circle-visible goals before returning them to connected couples. Shared goal information can include its steps, actions, and reflections; team calendar events are not shared with circles.

The Sharing screenshot proposes more granular switches than the current source enforces. Treat those controls as a design preview, not a released privacy guarantee.

Account and session controls

The API includes scrypt password hashing, hashed session/reset tokens, authentication throttling, membership checks, and session revocation. Native credentials use Expo SecureStore. These controls do not establish whole-workspace encryption or a completed security certification.

Reminders and permissions

Device reminders use optional local scheduling with generic text. Partner and circle notices appear after in-app synchronization; a remote background push service is not included in the reviewed source.

Release review remains in progress

The supplied security review records unresolved high-severity findings in the Expo/tooling dependency tree. Framework remediation or reviewed mitigations, signed mobile builds, store disclosures, and real-device accessibility and permission checks remain release work. No Apple or Google approval is claimed.
