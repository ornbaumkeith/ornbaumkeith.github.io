Exam simulation settings

The official ESDP_2025 exam: 75 items / 105 minutes = 84 seconds per item. The 60-question practice bank uses the same pace: 84 minutes.

All questions are independently authored training scenarios derived from documented concepts; they are not official Esri questions or a representation of exact exam wording or passing score.

Blueprint and evidence note. The official ESDP_2025 exam information guide identifies four weighted domains: Physical Architecture 35%, Solution Architecture 26%, Deployment and Troubleshooting 14%, and Architectural Concepts 25%. Advanced custom security architecture and complex load-balancer/network engineering are outside the stated qualified-candidate scope. The capacity examples are transparent illustrative calculations—not official Esri hardware formulas.

Physical architecture & decision quick-reference

These are design aids. Numeric CPU computations illustrate capacity reasoning; they are not Esri-prescribed per-service sizing constants.

Availability budget

Annual downtime (min) = 525,600 × (1 − availability)

99.9% allows approximately 525.6 minutes/year (assuming a 365-day year).

Illustrative request concurrency

Average in-flight = RPS × mean duration

For a steady workload, 40 RPS × 0.25 s ≈ 10 average in-flight requests. Benchmark real services.

Esri physical design guidance ↗
Surviving-node capacity

Peak capacity after any single-node failure ≥ required peak load

Test N−1 with realistic service and data loads, not merely successful ping/health checks.

Resiliency vocabulary

HA ≠ DR  |  RPO ≠ RTO

HA minimizes service disruption; DR restores larger outages; RPO is maximum data age and RTO is service return time.
