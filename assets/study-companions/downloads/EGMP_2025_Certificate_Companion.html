Exam simulation settings

The official exam: 75 items / 105 minutes = 84 seconds per item. This companion’s 60-question practice can use the same pace: 84 minutes.

Questions are original, documentation-derived practice. They are not official Esri exam questions and do not represent a guaranteed exam style, pass score or question pool.

Hands-on lab checklist

Use a nonproduction ArcGIS Pro/Enterprise lab with appropriately licensed access. Never test schema, maintenance or destructive commands on production.

Suggested six-week mastery schedule

Final-week strategy: Solve 60 questions at 84 seconds each; review every missed item against its cited Esri page. Repeat only after targeted practice; a better score caused by memorizing the same choices is not strong evidence of readiness.
