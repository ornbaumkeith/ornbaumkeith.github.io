ClearWorthPlanner

A clearer view of your financial life: income, expenses, savings, debt, and longer-term planning in one local-first Windows workspace.

WindowsPersonal financeLocal-firstVersion 1.3.1

Features & purpose

  • Configurable dashboards with planned/actual cash-flow diagrams, resettable category filters, budgets, and income allocation.
  • Planning Studio with separate actual, planned, and projected values; savings and debt goals.
  • Multi-view calendar workspaces, task planning, imports, reports, and transaction tracking under Import.

Technology

Electron, React, TypeScript, and SQLite, with Vite, ExcelJS, and Windows installer tooling.

Privacy & security

Argon2id password hashing, configurable session and lockout controls, role checks, Windows-protected secrets, and Electron isolation. Saved-secret protection does not establish whole-database or backup encryption.

Availability: Version 1.3.1 is available as an unsigned Windows installer. Optional Plaid bank linking currently has limited trial availability.

Pairvoy

Your goals. Our journey. Make goals and everyday habits a friendly challenge with your spouse, girlfriend, boyfriend, or partner—and find inspiration in other couples’ shared progress.

Windows & AndroidDesktop & phone layoutsSMART goalsRebranding in progress

Features & purpose

  • Personal and shared goals, editable milestones, scheduled actions, and calendar planning.
  • XP, levels, activity, and reflections to make consistency visible and hold each other accountable.
  • Connected couples can view shared goals, copy inspiration, and send positive encouragement.

Technology

React Native, Expo and TypeScript, with Electron for Windows and a Node.js/Express API supporting PostgreSQL and SQLite.

Privacy & security

Choose what connected circles can see, including planning steps, actions and reflections. Working notes remain within your core team. Testing and security review remain part of the release process.

Availability: Pairvoy 0.5.15 builds on the latest 0.5.14 functionality. Rebranded Windows and Android installers are being verified.
