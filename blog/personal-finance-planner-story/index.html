The Story Behind the Personal Finance Planner

Keith Ornbaum · October 23, 2026

My Personal Finance Planner started as a standalone Excel workbook, refined over years of use to organize income, expenses, and planning.

One place for financial details

Monthly worksheets and annual summaries offer a way to record and review transactions, while assets, debts, and accounts have their own dedicated views.

Refined over time

Keeping records across months makes it easier to review recurring expenses and plan ahead. I adjusted the layout and calculations over time based on how the workbook was used.

Independent resource

This spreadsheet stands on its own. The lessons from developing it later helped inspire ClearWorth, a separate software project; the planner is not part of that application.

Sharing the original

The workbook, screenshots, and videos are provided as supplied, without modification or generated replacements. Do not store real passwords in an unencrypted workbook.
