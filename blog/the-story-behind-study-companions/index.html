Study Companions: Turning a Personal Need Into a Resource for the GIS Community

Sometimes, the most rewarding projects begin with a personal need and evolve into something that can benefit an entire community.

The Inspiration Behind Study Companions

Throughout my career in GIS, I have developed a genuine appreciation for continuous learning, professional development, and staying current with technology.

Preparing for Esri technical certifications has reinforced that appreciation. With the depth and complexity of the ArcGIS ecosystem, preparing for a professional-level certification requires more than memorizing terminology. It requires understanding technical concepts, architectural decisions, best practices, troubleshooting, and how everything works together in real-world environments.

Esri provides a tremendous amount of valuable documentation, training, and technical resources. While preparing for my own certifications, I recognized an opportunity to bring those resources together in a more structured, interactive, and personalized learning environment.

I wanted something that would allow me to study at my own pace, challenge my understanding, identify areas where I needed improvement, and quickly reference technical information.

Rather than simply continuing with my existing study methods, I decided to build something that would complement them.

That is how Study Companions came to life.

Learning With Purpose

With almost two decades of experience working in GIS, databases, automation, and enterprise technology, I have learned that knowing how to perform a task is only part of being a well-rounded technical professional.

Understanding why something works, when to use it, and how it interacts with other components is equally important.

I wanted Study Companions to encourage that deeper level of understanding.

The objective was not simply to prepare for a certification exam. I wanted to create a learning environment that would help me become a more knowledgeable, capable, and well-rounded GIS professional.

I also wanted a resource that would remain valuable long after the exam.

Four Certification Study Companions

I have developed four independently accessible Study Companions organized around Esri's 2025 professional-level certification objectives.

Study CompanionPrimary Focus
ArcGIS Pro ProfessionalData management, mapping, visualization, sharing, and spatial analysis.
ArcGIS Enterprise Administration ProfessionalDeployment, configuration, administration, operations, and troubleshooting.
ArcGIS Enterprise Geodata Management ProfessionalEnterprise geodatabases, versioning, data integrity, editing, and maintenance.
ArcGIS Enterprise System Design ProfessionalEnterprise architecture, availability, scalability, performance, and disaster recovery.

Collectively, the four companions provide:

  • 400 technical questions and answers.
  • 400 interactive flashcards.
  • 400 independently developed practice exam questions.
  • More than 300 Esri documentation references.
  • Structured study guides and hands-on exercises.
  • Technical scenarios and progress tracking.

Each companion is designed to help users connect technical knowledge with practical application.

More Than a Traditional Study Guide

One thing I am particularly excited about is the interactive functionality built into each Study Companion.

Core Study Guides

Review major certification topics, technical concepts, architectural considerations, and recommended practices in an organized learning environment.

Interactive Flashcards

Practice recalling information rather than simply rereading it. Flashcards help reinforce technical concepts and identify areas that require additional attention.

Technical Questions and Answers

Explore technical scenarios involving troubleshooting, system administration, geodatabase management, architectural decisions, and other practical situations.

Practice Exams

Each companion includes 100 original practice questions, explanations, documentation references, and an optional 105-minute practice timer. These are independently developed exercises, not actual Esri certification exam questions.

Hands-On Exercises

Reinforce your understanding by applying technical concepts within an appropriate ArcGIS testing environment.

Progress Tracking

Monitor your study activities, review completed topics, and identify areas where additional preparation may be beneficial. Progress is stored locally in your browser.

Esri Documentation References

Access official Esri documentation directly from the study material. This is particularly valuable when researching a concept, validating technical information, or exploring a subject in greater detail.

Download. Open. Study.

I wanted these resources to be as accessible as possible without requiring complicated installations, subscriptions, accounts, or additional software.

Each Study Companion is a standalone, interactive HTML file built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Getting started is simple:

  1. Visit the Study Companions project page.
  2. Download the companion that aligns with your certification or professional interests.
  3. Open the downloaded file in Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, or another modern desktop browser.
  4. Review the study guide, work through the technical questions, practice with flashcards, and complete the practice exams.
  5. Monitor your progress and revisit areas where you need additional practice.

The core study functionality works offline, and each companion includes light and dark display themes.

An internet connection is only needed when accessing external documentation and resources. Progress is stored locally, with import and export functionality available where supported.

A Resource I Continue to Use

What began as a certification preparation project has become something I reference regularly in my professional work.

Whether I am reviewing enterprise architecture, exploring geodatabase functionality, researching an administrative task, or refreshing my understanding of a technical concept, I find value in having an organized collection of information and documentation references readily available.

That has become one of my favorite aspects of this project.

The value of learning should not expire when you complete an exam or earn a certification.

Technology continues to evolve, and even experienced professionals benefit from revisiting concepts, challenging their understanding, and expanding their knowledge.

Study Companions has become another tool in my professional toolbox that helps me do exactly that.

Giving Back to the GIS Community

Throughout my career, I have benefited tremendously from individuals who were willing to share their knowledge, experiences, technical resources, and lessons learned.

The GIS community has an incredible network of professionals who contribute to the growth and success of others.

Making Study Companions freely available is my opportunity to contribute something of my own.

My hope is that these resources help other GIS professionals feel more confident in their certification preparation, develop a deeper understanding of the ArcGIS ecosystem, and continue growing throughout their careers.

These companions are independently developed resources intended to complement Esri's official documentation, training, certification guides, and hands-on experience. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Esri, and they do not contain actual certification exam questions.

I encourage everyone using them to continue exploring Esri's official resources and validating technical concepts against current documentation.

Never Stop Learning

One of my personal philosophies is simple:

Outwork. Outlearn. Outlast.

To me, continuous learning is not about knowing more than everyone else. It is about being better equipped today than you were yesterday and remaining curious enough to continue improving tomorrow.

Study Companions represents that philosophy.

What started as something I needed for myself has evolved into something I am excited to share with others.

Whether you are preparing for an Esri certification, expanding your technical knowledge, or simply looking for an additional professional reference, I hope these resources provide you with the same value they have provided me.

Knowledge becomes even more valuable when we share it.
