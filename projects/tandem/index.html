From intention to action

Plan it. Show up. Celebrate it.

Goals with a clear purpose

Define the title, meaning, category, ownership, target date, and SMART measure of success. Search and filter goals by owner, category, status, and year.

Four steps to get started

Every new goal starts with Prep & Plan, Schedule & System, Track & Adjust, and Finish & Review. Rename, date, delete, restore, or expand these suggestions to fit your plan.

Actions and milestones

Use dated actions for everyday follow-through and planning steps for milestones. Check actions as you go; completing all planning steps completes the goal and awards its completion bonus.

A calendar around your goals

Bring goal deadlines, steps, actions, and shared events into a month view and daily agenda. Export calendar dates in .ics format for your preferred calendar.

Progress you can see

Switch between daily, weekly, monthly, and annual views of scheduled and completed actions. Home keeps next steps and active goals close at hand.

XP, levels and achievements

Journey brings together XP, level progress, completed steps, actions checked, finished goals, achievements, and recent moments.

People who keep you going

Circles brings your team, connected teams, and encouragement together. The sharing panel includes controls for goal visibility, planning steps, dated actions, and monthly reflections.

Reflection with context

Save a reflection for a selected goal and month. Activity also keeps recent events and completed or archived goals available for review by year.

A workspace that feels like you

Customize your profile, photo or avatar, appearance, date entry, and reminder preferences. Profile also provides personal backup, calendar export, and account/security entry points.

A little rivalry. A shared win.

Make consistency something to celebrate.

Small actions add up.

Earn 2 XP per checked action, 10 XP per completed step, and 50 XP per finished goal, with a 25 XP shared-goal bonus. Each 200 XP advances a level. Reopening completed work reverses its credit so repeated toggles do not multiply rewards.

Use the progress views for a friendly challenge with your partner, while keeping the focus on showing up and supporting one another.

Inspiration from your circle.

Connected teams can view shared goals, copy ideas, and offer encouragement. The sharing panel explains that connected teams cannot edit your original goals; your own teammates can collaborate on team goals.

Working notes stay within the core team, according to the interface. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Privacy & trust

Clear boundaries for shared progress.

Sharing with intention

Choose whether a goal is visible to connected circles, with separate controls for steps, dated actions, and monthly reflections. The server redacts private sections. Working notes stay with your core team; connected teams can view, copy, and encourage visible goals, but cannot edit your original plans.

Live API checks verified partner linking, two-way synchronization, private-section redaction, stale-edit rejection, replay protection, and persistence through a real service redeploy. This is API evidence, not physical-device certification. Previously downloaded copies cannot be recalled.

Calendar exports & reminders

Calendar exports include your team’s active goal, step, action, and event titles/dates. Notes and connected-circle data are excluded. An .ics file or copied calendar event is a snapshot; later goal changes do not update it.

Android reminder delivery requires operating-system permission and explicit scheduling. Windows uses in-app check-ins. Partner achievements appear through synchronized in-app activity; remote push notifications are not provided.

Testing limitations & account safety

These are pre-release testing packages. Windows is not Authenticode-signed and may show an unknown publisher warning. Android signing identifies a consistent update key; it is not an independent security certification.

Password-recovery email delivery is not configured. Use a password manager and test credentials; recovery requests currently fail safely rather than returning reset tokens. Encrypted off-service backup/restore and physical-device acceptance remain open. Dependency audit findings remain documented in the release notes; passing functional checks does not clear them.

The API uses Render Free and Supabase Free. Inactivity, service wake-up, pauses, and quotas can interrupt testing. No always-on availability or whole-workspace encryption is claimed. Avoid sensitive or irreplaceable data during this pilot.

Get started

  1. Download the matching package and checksum. Keep any existing Tandem data backed up.
  2. On Windows, run the setup EXE. On Android, open the APK and follow the installation prompt.
  3. Open Tandem, register a test account, and sign in. The server address is included: https://tandem-api-ornbaumkeith.onrender.com. Allow time for the free service to wake if needed.
  4. Use Profile’s team/connection access to invite your partner. Use a separate account on each device, then create a shared goal and confirm changes appear on both.

Data from an older local workspace is not automatically imported into a new cloud account.

What to test

Create a goal, edit its four suggested steps and dates, check an action, and confirm XP. Try the Calendar, monthly reflections, light/dark appearance, and a connected circle’s privacy controls. Reopen the app and check that saved changes remain.

Windows packaged-app launch and Android emulator results are recorded with the release. Physical phone/tablet and personal Windows installation checks remain tester acceptance tasks.
