A prospect, from boundary to field records

Keep the map and the work together.

ClaimField brings spatial planning, land-reference research, terrain analysis and crew coordination into one browser-based GIS workspace. Draw or import areas of interest, prepare claim geometry and work through the details needed for field and filing review.

Built for exploration teams, GIS and land professionals, and field coordinators, it connects the planning map with editable schedules, staking records and exportable documents. Proposed geometry and planned dates stay distinct from actual field observations and filing records.

The ClaimField workflow

From an AOI to a Prospect Summary.

  1. Prospect & AOI

    Name the prospect, choose the state and county, and draw or import one or more areas of interest. Generate and edit planning geometry.

  2. Land Search / Review

    Review surface-management context, mapped active claims, restrictions and PLSS references. Check source dates, coverage and unresolved records.

  3. Track Map & NOLs

    Prepare numbered staking points, field tracks and Notices of Location. Set access, start and finish points, then divide planned work among workers.

  4. County filing preparation

    Review Certificates of Location and filing maps with the required prospect facts. Confirm the applicable county recording instructions.

  5. BLM filing preparation

    Prepare a monochrome filing map and supporting records, review missing details, and record filing facts. Submission takes place through the appropriate agency process.

  6. Prospect Summary

    Review land, field and filing records together. Export a Prospect Package with generated maps, documents, schedules and a manifest of omissions.

Scheduling supports the field stage with Calendar, Timeline and List views. ClaimField prepares and organizes records; it does not submit filings or certify a valid mineral location.

What is in the current app

Land detail. Field coordination. Usable outputs.

Land Review across the map frame

Land Review requests surface-management context, mapped active claims and PLSS for the printed map frame, including beyond the AOI. Maps include north, scale, legends and source-status notes. Missing or partial coverage stays explicit.

Readable claims & staking points

Claim names run along the claim length. Track-map corners use numbered white squares with black outlines, discovery/site points use blue symbols, and tie points remain red crosses. Zoom and layout controls support review at different scales.

Terrain-aware crew tracks

Set optional access and start/finish points, choose a slope threshold and split work among workers. Background processing, cancellation and error recovery support generation. Sampled terrain and route estimates still require an access and safety review.

Interactive AOI elevation

Inspect area, low/median/high elevation and sampled terrain in 3D, overhead and side views. Rotate the grid, inspect samples, compare section profiles and export elevation data.

Calendar, Timeline & List

Coordinate prospects, crews and workers in linked schedule views. Move and resize visits, review availability and assignment conflicts, manage dependencies, and export calendar files or a summary PDF.

Map layouts & document review

Prepare Land Search / Review, color Track Map and monochrome filing outputs. Review NOLs, COLs and the Prospect Summary, choose sheet sizes, and inspect the live layout before export.

Tables, layers & field records

Inspect claim and loaded-feature attributes, search and select records, edit supported fields and export tables. Manage reference layers, markup, measurements and prospect attachments alongside the map.

Expiring encrypted snapshots

Create an AES-256-GCM snapshot with a 15-, 30- or 60-minute expiry. The first recipient who deliberately opens it gets a read-only view without an account. The sender can revoke access; later prospect edits and separate attachments are not synchronized.

Saved prospects & GIS exchange

Account-owned prospects, attachments and schedules use durable server storage. Browser drafts remain separate. Export prospect JSON, spatial data and QGIS/QField preparation packages for a reviewed GIS handoff.

What goes into a Prospect Package?

The Prospect Summary brings the generated land, field and filing records together. The downloadable ZIP includes an index, prospect data and a manifest so missing images, failed sources or omitted outputs can be reviewed.

Land & map records

  • Land Review map, summary, AOI and source-query log.
  • Loaded land layers and map-frame context.
  • Track and filing maps, generated from the prospect.

Field & filing preparation

  • Notices of Location and Certificates of Location.
  • Staking schedule, field-track CSV and worker checklist.
  • Claim and staking-point spatial records, saved crew tracks and Prospect Summary.

The ZIP contains generated prospect records. Separately uploaded account attachments are not included automatically; download and retain them separately. Review the manifest and source warnings before sharing or relying on a package.

Architecture & code

A spatial browser app with durable account storage.

The hosted 0.27.22 app uses a React/TypeScript frontend and the existing account API adapted to Cloudflare Workers with a SQLite-backed Durable Object. The local Node.js server remains a separate supported deployment path.

INTERFACE

Browser workspace

React, TypeScript and Vite connect prospect state, map tools, documents and scheduling. MapLibre GL renders the main map.

ANALYSIS

Geometry & terrain

Turf and GeographicLib support spatial calculations. Web Workers handle terrain routing; live map and elevation sources supply context.

PERSISTENCE

Worker & SQLite

A Worker routes account API requests to a Durable Object. SQLite stores accounts, prospects, schedules, attachment chunks and encrypted-share records.

Verified hosting behavior

API checks covered registration/login, account isolation, save conflicts, attachment parity, scheduling, encrypted-share redemption and revocation. Saved data, sessions and share state survived a real redeployment. These checks are not a full security certification or a backup-restoration drill.

Practical operating limits

The deployment is configured for Cloudflare’s free tier, with provider quotas and an application storage budget. Keep prospect exports and attachment backups. Complete browser/map acceptance, production recovery and sustained-load testing remain ongoing; password-recovery email is not configured.

Before your first prospect

Getting started, with the right expectations.

Is ClaimField a finished production release?

No. ClaimField is currently in Beta and is still being tested and improved. Features and behavior may change. Keep backups of important work and independently verify planning outputs before relying on them.

How do I access ClaimField?

Select Open ClaimField, choose Create account and sign in. Start with a test prospect and work through AOI, Land Review and Track Map preparation. The setup and testing guide includes validation and troubleshooting steps.

Will my local account and prospects be there?

Local and hosted accounts use separate stores. Export prospects from the local app, create your hosted account and import the prospect exports deliberately. Transfer attachments separately and keep the original local data intact.

What does Land Review establish?

It organizes mapped records and source coverage for review. Surface management is not mineral title; a private-or-unknown classification is not a verified parcel record. Mapped active claims do not establish operating mines or surveyed boundaries. Check current agency and county records, mineral estate, withdrawals and legal access independently.

Can a shared snapshot be copied?

Yes. Anyone holding the full link may attempt its one-time opening. Expiry runs from creation, and the sender can revoke access, but recipients can retain screenshots or copied information. Closing or reloading ends the consumed browser view. Only share with the intended recipient.

Is it an offline field app or an electronic filing service?

The hosted workspace needs connectivity for sign-in, server saves and live reference services. GIS export packages support separate field workflows after validation. ClaimField prepares planning documents and records; it does not automatically file them with a county or BLM, certify claim validity or establish safe physical access.

What browser and recovery steps should I plan for?

Use a current desktop browser with WebGL available for the main map, and review large prospects on a suitably sized screen. Device/GPU behavior can vary. Export important work regularly and retain separate attachment backups. Password-recovery email is not available in the current hosted setup.
