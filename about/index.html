Experience · Curiosity · Growth

Hi, I'm Keith.

My work connects enterprise GIS, data, automation, and practical problem solving. My personal projects connect that experience with the things I care about: family, learning, and building a stronger financial future.

ClearWorth grew out of more than ten years of work, learning, and personal growth. It brings together technical experience and an ongoing effort to make financial decisions clearer and more intentional. I also wanted to use what I have learned to help others, building apps and tools that make everyday life easier for everyday people.

What keeps me curious.

I am interested in enterprise GIS, location intelligence, databases, automation, and software design. I enjoy making complex information easier to understand and turning repeated tasks into useful workflows.

Outside technical work, family and youth sports coaching have been a meaningful part of my life. Coaching has reinforced the importance of preparation, communication, persistence, and continued learning.

What experience has taught me.

Work with ArcGIS Enterprise, SQL Server, and data workflows has shaped how I approach a problem: understand the inputs, make the process clear, test the result, and document how it works.

Personal growth brings another lesson: progress takes honesty about where you are, consistent effort, and a willingness to adjust. Those ideas influence both how I build and how I think about finances.

How the pieces came together

A journey toward ClearWorth.

Building a foundation

Learning through real work.

Years of technical projects developed my experience with data, systems, automation, and the needs of the people using them.

Broadening the perspective

Connecting work and life.

Family responsibilities, coaching, and continued self-learning strengthened my interest in practical tools, clear communication, and long-term thinking.

Making finances clearer

From tracking to planning.

My financial interests extend beyond recording expenses to understanding income, due dates, debt, savings, and how today's choices affect future goals.

Putting experience into practice

Developing ClearWorth.

ClearWorth brings those interests together in a local-first Windows application. Its ongoing development connects financial organization with the technical habits I have built over time.
